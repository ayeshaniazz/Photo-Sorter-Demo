# Photo Sorter (Demo)

Sort a messy folder into neat albums in seconds: selfies, food, pets, sunsets, screenshots, videos, duplicates, blurry photos, PDFs, spreadsheets and more.

![Photo Sorter sorting a folder](preview.gif)

## ▶ Try the live demo

**[Open the demo](https://ayeshaniazz.github.io/Photo-Sorter-Demo/)**

Press **Sort**, watch everything fly into its album, then click any album to look inside.

> This demo uses built-in sample photos. Nothing on your computer is touched.

## ♡ Get the full version

The full version sorts **your own folders**:

- Photos, videos and screenshots sorted into albums
- Duplicates, similar photos and blurry photos found for you
- PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations and Word documents grouped in one Documents folder
- **Copy** or **Move**, plus **Undo**
- Runs entirely on your computer, so your photos are never uploaded
